Country send windows

One shared campaign can send to all four countries at once — each lead is only sent to during its own country's window below, read from the country column in your sheet automatically. Each window is set in that country's own local time zone — no manual conversion needed. Turn this on per campaign with the "Respect per-country send windows" checkbox when creating or editing a campaign on the Campaigns page.
