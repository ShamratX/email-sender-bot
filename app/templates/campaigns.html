How personalization works

Type {{ '{{' }}Column Name{{ '}}' }} in the subject or body — it matches a column header from your uploaded sheet exactly, case-insensitive. Works with any sheet, any columns; nothing is fixed to one lead source.

Example — sheet with columns business_name, city, rating:

Subject: Quick question about {{ '{{' }}business_name{{ '}}' }}
Body: Hi {{ '{{' }}business_name{{ '}}' }}, I noticed your {{ '{{' }}rating{{ '}}' }}-star rating
in {{ '{{' }}city{{ '}}' }}. Worth a quick look?

Only requirement: an email column (any name containing "mail" is auto-detected). Every other column is optional — use what you need, skip the rest. If a template uses a variable your sheet doesn't have, saving is blocked with the exact missing name so nothing sends blank.
